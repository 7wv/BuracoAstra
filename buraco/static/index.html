    THE BASICS

    A turn at the table

    1. Draw. Take one stock card or the entire discard pile.
    2. Build. Select three or more cards for a same-suit run or ace set. Each meld allows at most one wild. Select cards and a target meld to extend it.
    3. Discard. Select one card and end your turn.

    Seven or more cards make a canastra: 200 points if clean, 100 if dirty. Cards on the table also score individually. Twos can be natural or wild; their positions can change when you add cards.

    Empty your hand to take your team's morto. If you discard to take it, wait for your next turn. Otherwise, keep playing. Going out may require a clean canastra.

    Only the active player's hand is shown. This is a shared-screen game; the next hand appears immediately after a discard.

    Queue melds to meet a vulnerable team's opening minimum in one play. An invalid play leaves the table unchanged.
